It all started in 2015 with a single espresso machine and a dream to bring the neighborhood together, one cup at a time. What began as a small corner spot has grown into a place where regulars know each other by name and the smell of freshly baked pastries welcomes you before you even open the door. We believe coffee is more than just a drink — it's a reason to slow down, catch up with a friend, or find a quiet moment before the day gets busy. Every bean we use is ethically sourced and roasted in small batches to bring out the flavors we're proud to serve. Ten years later, we're still doing what we love: pouring great coffee and creating a space that feels like home.
